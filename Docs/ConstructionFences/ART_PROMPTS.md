# Baustellenzaun-Grafiken

Erstellt mit dem eingebauten `image_gen`-Werkzeug, ohne CLI und ohne Raster-Nachbearbeitung. Die finalen PNGs behalten ihre erzeugte Transparenz.

## Modulares Zaunelement

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE seamless modular construction-site fence panel texture for a premium hand-painted Unity farming town game. Primary request: an attractive, substantial temporary construction fence that can be repeated horizontally and mapped onto upright planes around a building. STRICT STRAIGHT-ON ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT ELEVATION, absolutely horizontal rails and vertical posts, no perspective, no diagonally receding side face. Long landscape 3:1 composition. Dark desaturated teal tubular steel frame with thick brass clamps and bolts, taut chunky readable galvanized wire mesh in the upper two thirds, a solid narrow warm ochre safety board with charcoal diagonal hazard stripes across the lower third, a bottom steel rail. Warm upper-left painted highlights, tactile subtly worn metal, cozy illustrated industrial look matching a detailed hand-painted village, crisp silhouette and carefully painted depth on the tubing. Frame and horizontal rails continue flush to both left and right image edges with perfectly matching end profiles for seamless tiling; no end caps or protruding support feet. Keep all machinery within the canvas. Maximum useful detail. TRULY TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, INCLUDING ALL OPENINGS BETWEEN THE MESH WIRES and above/below the frame; the mesh must have clearly open holes showing the transparent background. No ground, no building, no scenery, no people, no logos, no lettering, no text, no watermark, no checkerboard. One panel only, no montage.

## Bereinigung der Transparenz

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached transparent fence texture. Keep the entire fence geometry, full-width horizontal composition, brass fittings, teal frame, dark silver mesh and ochre/charcoal hazard board exactly unchanged. Clean the alpha cutout for production use: remove ALL neon green, yellow, red, or orange speckles and colored fringes floating within mesh openings or around the silhouette. Each opening must be fully transparent and contain absolutely no colored pixels except the actual gray mesh wire edges. Keep crisp natural metal edges with subtle antialiasing and retain true transparent background. Do not add anything and do not change the shape or style.

Match a modular teal metal mesh construction fence with ochre and charcoal warning stripes. Polished cozy industrial illustration with warm upper-left sunlight, subtle patina, clear chunky forms, precise readable silhouette. Tall narrow 1:3 image composition, centered, post fills roughly 88 percent of height with transparent padding, useful high resolution. True transparent background, no scene, no ground surface, no people, no fence panels attached, no text, no numbers, no watermark, no checkerboard.
